D.R.R.A. is a distributed warning and response department. We turn unusual signals into verified alerts, coordinate containment, and preserve a complete record for the next team.

Every operation follows the same order: receive the report, verify the threat, protect civilians, secure the scene, and transfer the evidence without breaking the chain of custody.
